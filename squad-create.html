Create Squad

As captain, you create the squad. Share its join code with your players - a squad holds 4 to 5 members.

One active squad per user. Joining instead? Join with a code.

What happens next

1

Your join code is created

When you create the squad, it gets its own join code, shown on your squad page.

2

Share the code with your players

Your players join with the code. A squad holds 4 to 5 members.

3

Apply together

With at least 4 members, your squad can apply for a tournament from the tournaments page.
