About Us

PlayZyro is a PUBG gaming community. This website is the community's home online: one website for the whole community, with tournaments, schedules, upcoming events and winners, in a PUBG themed design.

Players sign up and log in with their own dashboard. A team captain creates a squad of 4 to 5 members, members join through a code, and the squad applies for tournaments through the website, instead of managing everything over WhatsApp.

The admin panel keeps the community organised: the admin sees every user and team, updates tournaments, adds upcoming events, and selects and assigns teams to tournaments. Assigned teams see their assignment, and the room ID and password at match time, in their own panel.

How It Works

1

Create your account

Sign up and log in to your own PlayZyro account.

2

Build your squad

Create a squad as captain, or join one with the squad's join code. A squad holds 4 to 5 players.

3

Apply for a tournament

Squads with at least 4 members can apply from the tournament page.

4

Get assigned

The PlayZyro admin reviews applications and assigns squads. Watch your dashboard for your tournaments.

5

Play at match time

Your Room ID and password are published on your dashboard at match time, visible only to your squad.

FAQ

What is PlayZyro?

PlayZyro runs PUBG community tournaments online: schedules, squads, assignments and winners in one place.

How many players are in a squad?

A squad holds 4 to 5 players. The captain creates the squad and shares its join code.

How do I join a squad?

Ask the captain for the squad's join code, then use Join a Squad while logged in.

Can I be in more than one squad?

Each player has one active squad.

How does my squad enter a tournament?

A squad with at least 4 members applies from the tournament page. The admin assigns squads, and assigned squads see the tournament on their dashboard.

When do we get the Room ID and password?

The admin publishes them at match time. They appear on the dashboard of assigned squad members only.

Does PlayZyro take payments?

No. PlayZyro does not take payments, deposits or withdrawals on this site.

How are winners announced?

Winners from finished tournaments are published in the winners section on this site.
