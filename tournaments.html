How Squads Work

  • A captain creates a squad and shares its join code. A squad holds 4 to 5 players.
  • Players join with the code while logged in. In this stage each player has one active squad.
  • A squad with at least 4 members applies for a tournament from the tournament page.
  • The PlayZyro admin reviews applications and assigns squads. Assigned squads see the tournament on their dashboard.
  • The Room ID and password are published at match time, on the dashboards of assigned squad members only.
